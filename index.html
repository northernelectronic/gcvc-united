HOW TO PLAY

🎯
SERVETap to lock your aim, then tap again to set power. Gold zone = tough serve. Too little = net, too much = out.
🙌
PASSYour player runs to the ball for you. Tap when the rings meet. Perfect = great pass. Late or early = shank. No tap = miss!
🤲
SETAnyone can set. Tap = front set. Swipe ⬅ or ➡ = set that way: behind you is a back set, the far side is a set across.
💥
SPIKEYour hitter jumps. Swipe ⬆ when the rings meet at the top. Tilt the swipe ⬅ ➡ to aim. Longer swipe = deeper. Tap = soft tip.
✋
BLOCKWhen they spike, tap to jump at the net. Time it with their swing!
